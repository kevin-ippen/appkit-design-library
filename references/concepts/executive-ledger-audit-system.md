## Brand & Style

The design system projects uncompromised financial stewardship, fiscal discipline, and quiet executive authority. Inspired by heritage accounting ledgers and refined operational audit frameworks, it translates the deliberate accountability of zero-based envelope budgeting into high-stakes retail and enterprise oversight.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Minimalism** with **Editorial Ledger Utility**. The interface evokes physical parchment stock, archival stationery, and master financial reports: warm, matte surfaces replace synthetic digital white, while hairline ink borders anchor structured data into unambiguous tabular bands. Visual noise is rigorously pruned to elevate precision: every audit defect, variance delta, and allocation target commands direct, frictionless comprehension.

The resulting emotional signature is calm, disciplined, and resolute—instilling complete trust among C-suite operators, store auditors, and regional controllers who require immediate balance clarity without visual ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, physical ledger harmony governed by deep forest ink tones and tactile paper surfaces:

- **Primary (`#133E2C` / Hunter Forest):** Acts as the foundational anchor for primary actions, navigational headers, active budget pills, and top-tier KPI aggregates. It carries archival permanence and calm authority.
- **Secondary (`#0F6B5A` / Deep Emerald):** Reserved for trend affirmations, reconciled ledger entries, positive variance margins, and primary interactive filters.
- **Tertiary (`#D9F0C6` / Celadon Tint):** A luminescent, desaturated mint used strictly for high-priority notification chips, row-level selection tints, balanced budget badges, and target attainment fills.
- **Neutral (`#F9F7F2` / Unbleached Parchment):** The global viewport canvas. It simulates high-grade, uncoated woven paper, eliminating harsh digital glare and reinforcing warm editorial prestige.

### Functional Audit Tones
- **Ledger Ink (`#122B26`):** The primary text and high-contrast figure color. Used instead of pure black to maintain harmony with the green spectrum.
- **Ledger Rule (`#E2DDD2`):** Crisp, single-pixel architectural grid lines that compartmentalize tabular data blocks.
- **Defect Alert (`#A63228` on `#FDF0EE`):** Muted cinnabar for audit discrepancies, variance overages, and critical VOC (Voice of Customer) defect escalations.
- **Audit Warning (`#966810` on `#FDF8EA`):** Warm antique gold for pending reviews and cautionary variance thresholds.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its refined structural geometry, crisp modern terminals, and open counters that remain effortlessly legible on dense handheld screens.

### Numerical Fidelity & Tabular Setting
Every numerical financial readout, audit score, currency string, and percentage must employ tabular figures via `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1`. This aligns decimals and monetary units strictly across rows, turning audit ledgers and defect breakdowns into instantly scannable matrices.

### Scale Application
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for total portfolio balance, aggregate store health scores, and key fiscal rollups. Tight negative letter-spacing prevents visual drift.
- **Ledger Numbers (`label-ledger-num`):** Dedicated typographic token for balance columns, allocation remaining figures, and VOC count indicators.
- **Labels (`label-sm`):** Rendered uppercase with generous tracking (`+0.04em`) to establish authoritative, archival category stamps over audit columns and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a **Structured Dense Canvas** calibrated for single-handed mobile governance with seamless tablet audit expansion.

### Rhythm and Spatial Rules
- **Base Grid:** An unyielding 4px baseline rhythm guides vertical cadence.
- **Canvas Margins:** Mobile viewports strictly maintain a 16px (`1rem`) outer margin, allowing maximum horizontal real estate for ledger tables and multi-column delta comparisons.
- **Dense Grouping:** Related financial line items (such as budget category, allocated amount, and activity bar) sit inside 8px (`0.5rem`) gaps, whereas distinct store audit categories divide across 20px (`1.25rem`) vertical bounds.
- **Reflow Architecture:** On mobile, ledger rows display as horizontally unified cards separated by hairline rules. On tablet devices, viewports snap to a 6-to-12 column fluid layout where master store ledger metrics pin alongside real-time VOC defect review streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons artificial multi-layered shadows in favor of **Tonal Ledger Stacking** and **Architectural Hairline Borders**.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Viewport Canvas):* `#F9F7F2` (Unbleached Linen).
  - *Tier 1 (Ledger Cards & Data Groups):* `#FFFFFF` (Parchment Paper), bound by crisp 1px borders in `#E2DDD2`.
  - *Tier 2 (Header Summaries & Quick Action Bar):* `#133E2C` (Deep Hunter), creating immediate focal grounding.
- **Outlines over Dropshadows:** Cards, sheets, and popovers utilize `1px solid #E2DDD2` rather than ambient blurring. This maintains the clean, documentary feel of a physical ledger book.
- **Focused Elevation:** When interactive overlays or audit drill-down trays elevate, they feature a low-opacity warm tint shadow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 43, 38, 0.08)`), preserving paper flatness while declaring modal prominence.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft Geometric** shape language (`roundedness: 1`), conveying structured ledger balance while remaining tactile and ergonomically modern on handheld touchscreens.

- **Data Blocks & Containers:** Cards, balance ledger tiles, and audit summaries take a disciplined `4px` (`rounded-sm`) radius, maintaining clean, book-bound edges.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Employ `8px` (`rounded-lg`), delivering subtle softness for thumb targets without sliding into playful circular geometries.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish ephemeral status indicators and filter toggles from rigid rectangular ledger cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Hunter Forest (`#133E2C`) background with crisp white typography. Height: 44px on mobile. Pressed state transitions cleanly to `#0F6B5A`.
- **Secondary (Ledger Action):** White parchment surface with a 1px `#E2DDD2` border and `#122B26` ink text.
- **Tertiary / Inline Audit Actions:** Ghost styling with a hunter-green underline indicator and compact 32px hit-height for fast inline ledger balancing.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Healthy / Reconciled:** Celadon tint (`#D9F0C6`) fill with `#133E2C` text. Used for "Balanced", "Audited", and "Zero Defect" states.
- **VOC Defect Alert:** Soft rose (`#FDF0EE`) fill, `#A63228` text, and a fine 1px border (`#F4C7C3`). Communicates non-conformance instantly without panic.
- **Under Audit Review:** Antique gold surface (`#FDF8EA`) with `#966810` text.

### Tabular Ledger Cards & Rows
- Alternating rows employ subtle `#F9F7F2` paper banding.
- Every ledger item features three spatial zones:
  1. *Category & Store Meta:* Left-aligned `body-md` bolded with trailing timestamp or defect code.
  2. *Progress Meter:* A slender 4px allocation bar showing remaining versus spent funds.
  3. *Monetary / Defect Tally:* Right-aligned `label-ledger-num` in tabular digits.

### Checkboxes & Audit Radios
- Square 18px frames with 2px corner radius in `#133E2C`.
- When checked, the background fills with `#133E2C` displaying a sharp parchment-white checkmark.
- Used in audit checklists for pass/fail verification per store department.

### Input Fields & Allocation Steppers
- Matte white background, 1px `#E2DDD2` border, 40px height.
- On focus: crisp 1px outline transition to `#133E2C` with zero fuzzy glow rings.
- Financial numerical inputs feature right-aligned tabular figures with a fixed prefix currency mark pinned in `#4D6B3C`.

### Domain-Specific: VOC Defect Tracker Tile
- A composite component designed for quick audits: displays a high-visibility defect severity chip at the top-left, store location code in `label-sm` at the top-right, defect description in `body-md`, and a bottom horizontal delta rail illustrating repeat incidents against weekly store benchmarks.

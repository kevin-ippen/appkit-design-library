## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, executive-grade marketing analytics environment rooted in warm editorial sophistication and technical clarity. Moving away from sterile, cold blue SaaS conventions, it pairs warm earthy pigments—burnt umber, baked terracotta, and golden wheat—against an organic warm cream canvas, grounded by structured deep navy charcoal ink. 

The aesthetic merges contemporary editorial minimalism with tactile precision:
- **Atmosphere:** Discerning, authoritative, humanized, and calm under high data density.
- **Audience:** Growth marketing leaders, CMOs, intelligence directors, and executive stakeholders needing instant clarity on complex demographic cohorts and Voice of Customer (VOC) sentiment channels.
- **Visual Stance:** Generous negative space, restrained dual-tone charts, tactile segmented pill meters, and tailored typography that balances high-impact geometric punch with effortless micro-data legibility.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen pigments against crisp structured darks to create an inviting yet focused analytical plane.

- **Primary (`#b4533c` / `#9c412b`):** Burnt terracotta anchor. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation markers, key trend lines, and dominant cohort highlights.
- **Secondary (`#1c2438`):** Deep navy charcoal ink. Serves as high-contrast structural text, dark executive panels, table headers, and primary data axes.
- **Tertiary (`#d4974c`):** Warm golden wheat ochre. Applied to secondary data series, benchmark comparisons, and neutral-positive metric callouts.
- **Neutral (`#faf8f5`):** Sand/cream background foundation. Softens visual fatigue while sustaining sharp legibility.
- **Specialized Channel Colors:**
  - `slate-blue` (`#5b6b8c`): Used for demographic segment secondary buckets and secondary chart series.
  - `forest-teal-accent` (`#0f5f57`): Reserved for positive VOC sentiment, statistical significance badges, and goal achievements.
  - `cream-border` (`#e5ded3`): Low-contrast hairline dividers that maintain clean division without optical noise.

## Typography

The type system blends the structural, rhythmic geometry of **Space Grotesk** for metrics, section heads, and analytical micro-labels with the balanced, open humanism of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body descriptions, tooltips, and contextual insights.

- **Numerics & Metrics:** Primary statistical callouts and values utilize `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures (`tnum`) enabled to ensure cohort alignment across data grids and financial indices.
- **Analytical Labels:** Metric labels, status indicators, and demographic badges apply `label-md` or `label-sm` in uppercase with expanded tracking for immediate scanning against dense dashboards.
- **Editorial Voice:** Section introductions and VOC summaries use `Plus Jakarta Sans` regular/medium weights to preserve an approachable narrative texture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a disciplined, responsive 12-column grid system resting inside an adaptive fluid shell.

- **Breakpoints & Grids:**
  - **Desktop (>=1200px):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` canvas margins and `1.75rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-column cohort tables and parallel metric panels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Analytics sidebars collapse into contextual sheet drawers.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric cards and demographic segments stack vertically.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strict multiples of 4px/8px reinforce data tables and timeline components. Card interiors use standard `space-lg` padding to keep individual analytic modules cleanly separated and breathable.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering, delicate structural borders, and subtle diffuse light rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#faf8f5` (warm sand/cream).
  - **Surface Container (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#ffffff` framed by a 1px border of `#e5ded3`.
  - **Elevated Interactive Layer:** `#ffffff` with a warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 36, 56, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(180, 83, 60, 0.04)`.
  - **Inset / Tray Wells:** `#f2eee7` with flat borders for filtered search boxes, tabular subtotals, and progress channels.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Hairline borders (`#e5ded3`) maintain crisp modular segregation without creating high-contrast grid cages. Overlays (dialogs and context popovers) employ an ultra-soft navy-tinted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(28, 36, 56, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The shape system embraces a deliberate contrast between structured metric containers and soft, pill-shaped analytical indicators.

- **Cards & Data Grids:** Use standard rounded corners (`0.5rem` / `8px`) providing calm architectural stability for charts and tables.
- **Segmented Pills & Badges:** Demographic meters, VOC tag chips, filter tabs, and progress units use full pill shapes (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). This balances the technical grid with humanized, approachable organic forms.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form inputs, date pickers, and split-action buttons adhere to `0.5rem` radii, harmonizing with container envelopes.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Button:** Background `#b4533c`, text `#ffffff`, hover `#9c412b`. Compact vertical padding, Space Grotesk `label-lg`, `0.5rem` radius. Soft tactile feedback on active state (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e5ded3`, text `#1c2438`. Hover background `#f2eee7`.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent surface, text `#5b6b8c`, hover text `#1c2438` with subtle cream underlay.

### Chips & Filter Segment Tabs
- **Segment Filter Pill:** Resting on `#f2eee7` well. Active selection transforms into an elevated `#ffffff` pill with a micro-shadow and `#1c2438` text. Inactive tabs use `#475169` text with no border.
- **VOC Sentiment Tags:** Pill badge with full circular radius (`9999px`), light tinted fills (e.g., `#0f5f57` at 10% opacity with solid `#0f5f57` text for positive; `#b4533c` at 10% for churn risk), accompanied by micro-dot indicators.

### Demographic & Progress Segment Bars
- **Multi-Segment Track:** Composed of connected rounded pill segments separated by a 2px gap (`#ffffff`). 
- **Palette Fill:** Color-coded by demographic tier (Primary Terracotta `#b4533c`, Slate Blue `#5b6b8c`, Golden Wheat `#d4974c`, Dark Ink `#1c2438`).
- **Data Callout:** Embedded Space Grotesk `label-sm` percentages rendered directly above or within segments when dimension allows.

### Cohort Comparison Cards & Data Tables
- **Cards:** White background, 1px border `#e5ded3`, internal `space-lg` padding. Header features uppercase `label-md` tracking above a bold `metric-stat` display with delta variance arrows.
- **Table Structure:** Clean zebra-free rows with bottom borders in `#e5ded3`. Headers styled in `Space Grotesk` uppercase `label-sm` with charcoal ink `#475169`. Numeric columns right-aligned with fixed tabular figures.

### Form Inputs & Search Wells
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e5ded3`, text `#1c2438`, placeholder `#7a8396`. Focus state applies a 1px border in `#b4533c` and a subtle 2px terracotta tint ring (`rgba(180, 83, 60, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. Inactive border `#e5ded3`; checked state fills with `#b4533c` displaying a crisp white icon.

### Executive VOC Intelligence Stream
- **Feedback Modules:** Left-accented card utilizing a 3px terracotta or teal vertical signature edge, followed by editorial review quotes, attribution pills, and multichannel source icons (Email, Social, CRM).
